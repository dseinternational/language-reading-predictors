Its supporting observations still stand — the wave-3 domains really are near-collinear, the dominant eigenvalue really does carry ≈ 89.5 % of the variance, and raising `target_accept` to 0.999 really did starve the chains. What was wrong was the causal attribution: those divergences came from the **unidentified standard deviations inside `LKJCholeskyCov`**, not from the near-singular correlation matrix. The builder discarded both the Cholesky factor and the sds at construction (`_, corr, _`), and in a CFA the factor scale is fixed by the loadings, so those components were unidentified — they wandered, mixed badly, and, because the convergence gate scans every free RV, failed the gate on behalf of a parameter nothing downstream reads. Every R-hat/ESS failure across all four fits sat on `factor_cov` alone while every reported quantity converged cleanly (`factor_corr` R-hat ≤ 1.003 with ESS 2.2 k–24 k). Raising `target_accept` made it worse precisely because a smaller step size explores an unidentified direction more thoroughly, not less.

The off-diagonal correlations rise by 0.001–0.028 absolute, up to 0.23 posterior SD — roughly ten times the Monte-Carlo error implied by their old ESS — so the better-conditioned fits should be treated as authoritative rather than as a cleaner sample of the same posterior. # mm-001 sampling-quality gate exception (#412)

**Decision (2026-07-24, #412):** `lrp-rlm-mm-001` carries a recorded, signed-off
sampling-quality gate exception waiving the **divergences** check only. R-hat, ESS
and BFMI stay live, so any refit that regresses on mixing still fails the gate. The
exception is declared on `SPEC.extra["gate_exception"]`, applied at the single
chokepoint (`reporting._convergence_gate_failures`), and surfaced as a third badge
state and a `gate_exception` key-findings status — never a clean green pass, and
never by editing `diagnostics_summary.json` (whose `passed: false` stays the measured
truth about the sampler).

## Why the divergences are intrinsic, not a fixable fault

The wave-3 domains are near-collinear (factor correlations 0.81–0.95), so the LKJ
correlation matrix sits against the positive-definite boundary and leaves residual
divergences even after the communality reparameterisation removed the loading–residual
Heywood ridge (143 → 72 divergences). Pushing `target_accept` from 0.99 → 0.999 cut
the divergences to ~12 but starved the chains (min ESS 749 → 99, max R-hat 1.006 →
1.045), so 0.99 is the better operating point: the deliverables (loadings,
communalities, factor correlations) converge cleanly (R-hat ≤ 1.01, ESS ≥ 400) and
only the intrinsic near-singular-correlation divergences remain.

## Evidence (probe: `notes/assets/202607241200-mm001-gate-exception-probe.py`)

The probe reports, from a saved `trace.nc`:

- **Eigenvalue spectrum.** The first eigenvalue of the domain-correlation matrix
  carries ≈ **89.5%** of its variance (89% CI 85.4–92.7) — at wave 3 these four
  domains are close to a single dimension. The _smallest_ eigenvalue stays near zero
  throughout the posterior, which is the near-singularity that produces the
  divergences. This is a reason to **report the correlation matrix and its dominant
  share explicitly**, not to replace it.
- **Divergence-influence test.** Recomputing the factor correlations and communalities
  with the divergent draws excluded leaves them unmoved at reporting precision — the
  divergences do not contaminate the stored summary.
- **Divergence clustering.** The divergent draws are diffuse across chains, not
  concentrated in one region.

## What this establishes — and what it does not

It rules out the **crude** failure mode: divergent draws contaminating the posterior
summary. It does **not** rule out the substantive risk — posterior mass the sampler
never reached (divergences flag regions the trajectory failed to enter, so that bias
would show as _missing_ mass, not bad stored draws). That risk is covered by the other
two lines of evidence together: the `target_accept=0.999` run explored the space under
different dynamics and returned the same estimates, and the deliverables reach R-hat
1.006 across six chains. The exception rests on the invariance result **and** the
mixing **and** the divergence-influence probe, not on any one alone.

## Report obligations (mm-001)

The report must state plainly that these are **latent** correlations, disattenuated for
measurement error, and therefore sit systematically **above** the observed inter-test
correlations a reader would compute from raw scores; that every memory-domain
correlation scales with the assumed single-indicator reliability of 0.8 (`basdig` is
the only memory indicator in the prepared extract); and it must report the
**dominant-eigenvalue share** explicitly rather than leaving near-unidimensionality to
be inferred from six pairwise numbers.

## Sign-off condition (before merge)

Re-run the probe against **this branch's reporting-tier trace** and confirm: the
smallest eigenvalue is still near zero throughout the posterior, the divergences remain
unclustered, and excluding them still leaves the correlations and communalities unmoved
at reporting precision. If the residual divergences on the reparameterised reporting-tier
fit **do** cluster, the exception is not justified and this decision should be revisited.

**Which trace to use.** The sign-off fit must be run at the committed
`target_accept=0.99` — the ~72-divergence operating point. Do **not** reuse the
reporting output currently on disk: that is the earlier `target_accept=0.999`
exploration fit, which additionally fails R-hat and ESS (min ESS ≈ 99, max R-hat
≈ 1.045) and so is not the operating point. Running the fresh `0.99` fit also
regenerates `config.json`/badge/key-findings with the `gate_exception` applied.

**Probe validated (directional, not the sign-off).** Run against the on-disk
`0.999` trace the probe executes correctly and returns: dominant-eigenvalue share
0.905; smallest eigenvalue median 0.028 (5th pct 0.008, never negative);
12 divergences spread across chains ([1, 8, 0, 0, 2, 1]); and max |Δ| = 0.0000 for
both factor correlations and communalities when the divergent draws are excluded.
Consistent with the rationale, but the formal sign-off still requires the `0.99`
(72-divergence) fit above.

## Out of scope

- **mm-002** fails on R-hat 1.048 / min ESS 64 with **zero** divergences, so a
  divergence waiver does not touch it. The route there is a parameterisation change
  (`LKJCorr` + `L @ Lᵀ`, as the longitudinal correlated-factor family already uses),
  tried as separate work — not a family-wide waiver.
- The `Beta(2, 2)` communality prior is accepted as the default, pending a short
  prior-sensitivity sweep over its shape parameters (separate work) showing the
  loadings and communalities are not materially prior-driven at this sample size.
