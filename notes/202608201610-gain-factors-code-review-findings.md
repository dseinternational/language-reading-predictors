The review found one report-prose defect visible in published output (finding 1), one estimand/computation mismatch in a secondary descriptive table (finding 2), one silent selection bias in the key-findings highlight (finding 3), and a handful of minor observations. **All three findings are fixed in the change accompanying this note**, with tests pinning each fix, and the affected stored `-reporting` artefacts were refreshed in place (details per finding); the minor observations are recorded with no code change.

## Finding 1 — the off-floor fits' treatment marginal renders as "items" in the report partial (fixed)

`_results_factors.qmd`'s items-scale treatment-marginal block (both its primary and moderation-variant branches) has no off-floor branch. For the off-floor fits the pipeline correctly computes `treatment_marginal.csv` with `n_trials = 1`, so `trt_items_*` there is a **risk difference** in the probability of being off the floor — but the partial prints it unconditionally as `**{value:+.1f} items**`. The rendered `lrp-rli-gf-005` report therefore says the treatment average marginal effect "is a median of **-0.0 items** (50% CrI -0.0 to +0.0; 89% -0.1 to +0.1)" — a risk difference of −1.2 percentage points rendered with a wrong unit and rounding that destroys the number entirely — while the ROPE card three paragraphs below renders the same quantity correctly as "−1.2 percentage points (risk difference)". The two sentences sit in the same report and disagree about both unit and magnitude. Affected fits: gf-005, gf-011 (primaries) and gf-205, gf-211 (moderation variants); the treated-only gf-105 is unaffected (no treatment marginal). The key-findings builder (`_kf_build_gain_factors`) and the prior-pushforward sentence both branch on the off-floor flag correctly, so this is isolated to the partial. **Fix applied:** the block now branches on the plan's `off_floor` flag, and both the primary and moderation-variant sentences share one value formatter that renders whole-number percentage points ("on the chance of being off the floor at the period end"), with the key-findings builder's `round(...) or 0.0` idiom so a hair-negative value cannot render as "-0". A source-pinning test (`test_results_factors_partial_renders_off_floor_marginal_in_percentage_points`) asserts the branch exists, both branches use the shared formatter, and the raw one-decimal items f-string is gone. The four affected reports (gf-005, gf-011, gf-205, gf-211) were re-rendered in place with the repository `_partials` synced into each fit directory first.

## Finding 2 — the off-floor `own` association marginal computes a forward shift, not the documented at-floor → off-floor switch (fixed)

For the off-floor fits, the `own` row of `association_marginals.csv` is documented — in `AssociationTerm.perturbation_label`, in the pipeline's `_gf_association_terms` docstring, and in the table's own `scale` column ("off-floor at pre (0 to 1)") — as the at-floor → off-floor **switch** of the binary off-floor-at-pre indicator. What `association_marginals` actually computes is a uniform **forward shift**: `expit(η + γ) − expit(η)` averaged over **all** rows, including the rows whose indicator is already 1. For those rows the observed `η` already contains `γ·1`, so the computation evaluates an out-of-support 1 → 2 move at a flattened point on the logistic curve, diluting the average. The correct computation for a binary regressor is the net-out-and-toggle idiom the treatment marginal already uses (`η₀ = η − γ·x`, then `expit(η₀ + γ) − expit(η₀)`). Recomputed from the stored gf-005 trace: the saved row's median is **+0.279** while the documented toggle estimand is **+0.360** — a ~29% understatement, with 34% of the fitted rows off the floor at pre. Direction and the qualitative reading are unaffected; the number is not the number the label promises. The forward-shift convention is fine (and documented as the estimand) for the continuous covariates; only the binary indicator misdescribes. Affected artefacts: the `own` row of `association_marginals.csv` for gf-005, gf-011, gf-105, gf-205, gf-211. **Fix applied:** `AssociationTerm` gains a `toggle_vector` field (the observed 0/1 indicator, aligned with `eta`'s rows); when set, `association_marginals` nets the observed contribution out per row — exact, because the main effect and any interaction product are linear in the indicator — and contrasts the full 0 → 1 switch at that baseline, mirroring `_itt_ame_draws`; a `+k items` request on a toggle term is rejected loudly as incoherent, as is a wrong-length vector. The gain pipeline passes the off-floor indicator through, so the trt × own moderation variants net and switch the full per-row contribution too. Three tests pin the fix against explicit-loop references (`test_association_marginals_binary_toggle_*`), including the requirement that the toggle differs from the forward shift when some rows are already at 1 and coincides when none are. The five stored `association_marginals.csv` artefacts were then backfilled without a refit (model rebuilt without sampling; fitted-row identity checked against `config.json`; every non-`own` row verified to reproduce the stored numbers to ~1e-16 before overwriting, with the originals backed up): the `own` `prob_median` moved +0.279 → +0.360 (gf-005), +0.294 → +0.382 (gf-105), +0.280 → +0.363 (gf-205), −0.029 → −0.028 (gf-011) and −0.031 → −0.030 (gf-211) — material for the phonetic-spelling fits, negligible for nonword reading, whose indicator association is near zero. The affected reports were re-rendered.

## Finding 3 — the key-findings highlight cannot pick TR, TE or N baselines (fixed)

`_kf_strongest_factor` selects the most clearly resolved adjusted association by `|prob_positive − 0.5|` but only among terms present in `_KF_FACTOR_LABELS`, which lacks `gamma_TR`, `gamma_TE` and `gamma_N` — skill baselines fitted by gf-001, gf-002, gf-003, gf-006, gf-008, gf-010, gf-012 and gf-013 (and their companions/variants). Unlabelled terms are silently skipped, so the "most clearly resolved link" sentence can name a weaker association than the model actually found. This is live in published output: `lrp-rli-gf-008`'s key-findings box highlights the own baseline (P = 0.9988) although `gamma_TR` (P = 0.9995) is the most clearly resolved association in its own `factor_summary.csv`. The practical distortion in that instance is negligible (both are saturated), but the mechanism is a silent bias toward the labelled subset, and taught-vocabulary baselines are exactly the terms several of these models exist to surface. The docstring's stated reason for skipping unlabelled terms ("the box must stay readable without a code key") justifies skipping the `_missing` nuisance indicators and interactions, not omitting three skills that have perfectly good plain-language names. **Fix applied:** `_KF_FACTOR_LABELS` gains `gamma_TR` ("taught receptive vocabulary at the start of the period"), `gamma_TE` ("taught expressive vocabulary at the start of the period") and `gamma_N` ("nonword reading at the start of the period"), pinned by `test_gain_factors_highlight_can_pick_taught_vocabulary_baseline` (a factor summary where `gamma_TR` out-resolves `gamma_own` must produce the taught-vocabulary highlight). `key_findings.json` was regenerated via `scripts/regenerate_key_findings.py` for all thirty-two stored gain-factor reporting fits: exactly one box changed — gf-008's highlight now names taught receptive vocabulary, as the review predicted — and the other thirty-one regenerated identically. The gf-008 report was re-rendered.

## Minor observations (no code change made)

**Stale docstring on `resolve_active_interactions`.** The docstring still says the treated-only `b` companions "still _declare_ those pairs, on purpose — it keeps each companion a one-line diff from its parent". Since the #391 finding 3 decision, `GainFactorsModelSettings.__post_init__` rejects any `trt` pair on a non-moderation spec — treated-only included — and `test_treated_only_spec_declaring_trt_interactions_is_rejected` pins exactly that. At the plan level the treated-only filter is therefore unreachable (declared and active sets are always equal for validated settings; the recipe's "declared but not fitted" branch is dead there), and the docstring describes a state the validator forbids. The filter itself is still live for raw factory callers and should stay; the docstring should describe the current contract.

**Stale δ comment in the pipeline.** `pipelines/gain_factors.py` still says "F/T have no agreed delta and are skipped". F and T were adopted at δ = 1 item on 2026-07-20 and ratified with the ½-natural-maturation rule on 2026-08-19 (`measures.ROPE_DELTA` carries both), so the ROPE path does run for gf-007/gf-008 and the comment misdescribes the code below it.

**Interaction self-pairs are not rejected.** Neither the settings validation nor the factory rejects a self-pair such as `("own", "own")`. If one were declared, the factory would build the squared standardised term labelled as an interaction, and `_gf_association_terms` / `association_marginals` would compute its perturbation contribution wrongly (the shift of `z²` under `z → z + Δ` is `2zΔ + Δ²`, not `zΔ`). No registered model declares one; a one-line distinctness check in `_tuple_of_pairs` (or `__post_init__`) closes the gap cheaply.

**Legacy `extra` declarations.** All thirty-two gain-factor model modules still declare settings through `extra` rather than typed `GainFactorsModelSettings`; the strict `from_legacy_extra` translator rejects unknown keys before any directory reset, so this is safe, but every fit records `settings_source="legacy_extra"`. Migration to typed declarations is a mechanical follow-up, deferred (same posture as the DiD family note of the same date).

**Sweep point-estimate convention.** `scripts/gf_prior_sensitivity.py` records the sweep cells' items translation as a posterior **mean** (`items_mean`, correctly so named) where the family's headline artefacts lead with the median. The release decision itself classifies on the power-scaling statistics, not on this column, so nothing turns on it; noted so nobody reads the sweep column as the headline statistic.

## Verified sound (for the record)

**The on-intervention coding matches the delivered sessions cell-for-cell.** `OnIntervention = (G==1) | (phase>=1)` was checked against the raw session data (`attend`, read at each transition's pre row): the immediate arm averaged 72.7 / 64.8 / 52.0 sessions across the three periods and the waitlist 0 / 73.3 / 56.5 — so the immediate arm really was on intervention in all three periods and the waitlist from period 2, exactly as coded. (An earlier hypothesis during this review — that the immediate arm's period-3 transition was post-programme, which would have made the treated-only companions' "gains while on intervention" framing partly wrong — was checked against these data and is **false**; recorded here so it is not re-raised.) Because every period-2/3 row is on-intervention, `beta_trt` is likelihood-identified only by the period-1 between-arm contrast given the free per-period intercepts, exactly as the docstrings claim, and the marginal effect is correctly averaged over the period-1 rows only (`row_mask = phase == 0`).

**The AME core is correct and consistent.** `_itt_ame_draws` nets the full per-row treatment contribution out (`η₀ = η − δ·G`, with `δ = beta_trt + Σ gamma_int_trt_k·z_k` for the moderation variants) and toggles it back on; the moderator vectors handed over via the typed `GainFactorsPayload` are the exact standardised vectors the factory multiplied into `η`; non-treatment interactions correctly stay inside `η` and cancel. Row-mask validation fails loudly on shape/dtype misuse. `treatment_marginal.csv` and `rope_summary.csv` agree to the last digit on the stored gf-001 fit (both 2.6257 items), and the direction fields follow the AME rather than the coefficient (`direction_from_ame=True`), which matters exactly when trt interactions are fitted.

**The declaration-time validation is genuinely load-bearing.** Unknown keys, malformed pairs, undeclared interaction vocabulary, trt pairs on headline or treated-only specs, moderation variants without a trt pair or combined with `treated_only`, and outcome-as-own-skill all fail before `make_context` resets an output directory; the settings vocabulary and the factory's term set are pinned against each other by source-inspection tests, so they cannot drift silently.

**Covariate timing follows the #247 decision.** Speech (`deapp_c`) and phonological memory (`erbto`), with their `_missing` indicators, are routed to the pre-randomisation t1 baseline and broadcast; hearing (`hs`) stays contemporaneous at the post row; the ability covariate loads at t1. Constant-on-fitted-rows covariates are dropped by the loader, re-filtered in the pipeline, and the requested-vs-fitted sets are recorded via `effective_adjustment` in `config.json`.

**The off-floor parameterisation matches the #391 finding 2 decision and recovers truth.** The graded `gamma_own` (Normal(1, 0.25) on the raw pre-logit) is replaced on the Bernoulli path by the always-on binary off-floor-at-pre indicator with its own Normal(0, 1) prior, used identically for the main effect and any interaction naming `own`; the sampler-backed recovery test reproduces the generating parameters through the real factory.

**Gate, release and labelling discipline are coherent.** The convergence gate scans the model's free RVs (the per-child `u_child_raw` vector included) combined with the curated headline terms. `release.gate_applies` correctly exempts treated-only companions (no `beta_trt` exists to gate — "structurally not present" is distinguished from "not measured") and moderation variants (whose `beta_trt` is never released as causal); `causal_term_for` returns `beta_trt` for the gated fits. The moderation variants are consistently associational across every surface checked: the resolved plan's estimand/causal-status text, `factor_summary`'s roles (no causal row), the priors-table role demotion with its rationale, the report partial's branching prose, and the key-findings builder. The treated-only companions re-standardise the ability covariate on their kept rows while deliberately keeping age on the shared load-time scale for cross-variant comparability (#273), exactly as documented.

**Loader fail-loud checks hold.** Duplicate `(subject_id, time)` keys, within-subject group changes, fractional or negative counts, and ceiling violations all raise before a model is built; the `G = 2 − group` sign convention gives "positive = intervention benefit" throughout.
