## The did treatment-prior sweep (new `scripts/did_prior_sensitivity.py`)

The #482 gate withholds a prior-dominant focal claim unless "a `tau_prior_sensitivity.csv` treatment-prior sweep, computed from this fit's own trace" shows one sign across the grid, and four did fits shipped `robustness_unresolved` because no did runner existed: `did-001` (W) and `did-013` (W) on `tau_t2`, `did-003` (B) on `tau_t2`, and `did-007` (L) on its dose model's own focal slope `mu_dose`. The runner extends the level-family pattern (#488) with the shared attach discipline now lifted into `sensitivity.py` (`assert_primary_sampling_contract`, `attach_outcome_bundle`, `persist_sensitivity_trace`) so the level and did runners share one implementation rather than a third copy, and with three did-specific properties:

- **Keyed by model id, not outcome.** Two withheld fits share outcome W, so an outcome-keyed map cannot name them both and outcome identity alone cannot bind a bundle to its primary. The attach step now also verifies `primary_model_id` — a strengthening the level runner inherits.
- **Cells reproduce the primary's own sampling contract.** The preset must match on draws/tune/chains, and each cell _adopts_ the primary's recorded `target_accept` — `did-007`'s registered spec overrides the preset at 0.97, and evidence for that fit must be sampled under that fit's contract, not refused for having one.
- **The focal grid follows the plan's `effect_term`.** The `tau_t2` fits ride the proximal tau grid (0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75); `mu_dose` rides 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 around its `Normal(0, 1)` default — the same half/default/1.5× geometry. Items columns use the family's own translations: the wave-standardised t2 arm gap (`did_summary`) for arm-by-wave fits, the +1 SD session pushforward (`dose_marginal_summary`'s definition) for dose fits.

**Results (reporting tier, 2026-08-07): every swept fit keeps one sign across its grid.** `tau_t2` posterior means (logits): `did-001` +0.20 / +0.34 / +0.39, `did-013` +0.20 / +0.34 / +0.38, `did-003` +0.25 / +0.41 / +0.48 — the familiar monotone attenuation-release as the prior loosens, direction stable. `did-007`'s `mu_dose` barely moves at all: +0.11 / +0.13 / +0.13 per SD of sessions across a three-fold prior-scale range. All bundles are trace-backed (digest-verified cell traces installed beside each primary, `trace_file` rewritten, the gate's own evidence check asserted post-install) and **all four withholds resolve to `qualify`** with the standard prior-informed note — the exact evidence the gate named, measured rather than waived.

**One sampler decision worth recording.** `did-007`'s cells produced divergence-only failures at the primary's 0.97 across two seeds (6/36,000 draws in one cell under the first seed; 1 and 25 under a reseed — every other diagnostic clean, R-hat ≤ 1.0012, ESS ≥ 4,542), so the model's hierarchy retains divergence-prone geometry that seed choice shuffles between cells. Per-cell seed selection would have been evidence-shopping; instead the runner gained `--cell-target-accept`, which may only _raise_ the cells' target-accept above the primary's (stricter integration does not change the posterior; a looser sampler would relax the very contract the runner enforces). The attached `did-007` bundle is one coherent sweep at 0.99, recorded per row beside the primary's own 0.97, with all three cells at 0 divergences and estimates identical to the failed attempts to two decimals — the posterior was always stable; the integrator needed more care.

**A repair the sweep surfaced:** the key-findings builder's dose-companion detection keyed on a `beta_dose*` column, which the _period-varying_ dose model does not emit (its slopes live in `dose_slope_summary.csv`), so regenerating `did-007` crashed into the "predates the arm-by-wave schema" unavailable path and dropped its release decision entirely — the shipped report was publishing a zero-sentence findings box. Detection now also accepts the family's own `dose_interpretation` marker; `did-007`'s box renders the honest dose-association wording (3 sentences) plus the qualify note.

## Condition 1 — the independent-prior intercept fit (LRPDID101)

The 2026-07-24 option-B ruling retained the arm-by-wave empirical-Bayes intercept anchor with three conditions; conditions 2–3 (labelling, the stated prior-predictive limitation, the growth/LCSM survey) landed in #481. Condition 1 required a sensitivity fit with a **genuinely independent** prior — "not a wider σ around the same anchor — widening σ leaves the mean data-dependent, which is the actual defect". `LRPDID101` is that fit, registered as a companion of `did-001` (W): identical in every respect except `use_intercept_anchor=False` (a typed setting through `DiDModelSettings` → plan → factory), which replaces the anchored `alpha_anchor + alpha_offset` with the ordinary free `alpha ~ Normal(0, 1.5)` the dose variants already use. The setting is refused on dose models (their intercept is already free, so the claim would be a no-op), and the #481 labelling machinery keys on the `alpha_offset` name, so the companion's priors table and prior-predictive note carry no anchored-prior disclosure — correctly, since there is nothing to disclose.

**The demonstration is clean.** The reporting fit passes the full gate outright (0 divergences, max R-hat 1.0017, min ESS 3,603), and against `did-001`:

| quantity                                                 | `did-001` (anchored)  | `did-101` (independent prior) |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| `tau_t2` (logits, median, 89% ETI)                       | +0.34 [−0.05, +0.73]  | +0.32 [−0.07, +0.70]          |
| `tau_t2` (items)                                         | +2.22 [−0.31, +4.69]  | +2.09 [−0.44, +4.56]          |
| P(`tau_t2` > 0)                                          | 0.920                 | 0.908                         |
| `arm_gap_t1` / `arm_gap_t3` / `delta_crossover` (logits) | −0.14 / +0.23 / +0.11 | −0.17 / +0.21 / +0.10         |

The randomised contrast moves ~0.02 logits (≈ 0.13 items, about 5% of its posterior SD) — Monte-Carlo-error territory, exactly the "limited practical influence on `tau_t2`" the review predicted but required to be **demonstrated rather than assumed**. The anchor locates the level; it does no material work on the t2 contrast, which sits a full wave away from the t1 anchor.

Two corroborating observations. First, `did-101`'s own `tau_t2` power-scaling flags prior-dominant at essentially the parent's values (prior 0.058 / likelihood 0.033 vs 0.053 / 0.031) — the prior-dominance is the τ tier prior at n ≈ 54, not the anchor. The companion therefore joined the sweep set, keeps one sign across its grid (+0.19 / +0.32 / +0.37), and qualifies on the same trace-backed evidence as its parent. Second, the free-intercept fit's prior-predictive check is a genuine _location_ check — the diagnostic strength #481's limitation note records the anchored fits as giving up — and it passes. The growth and LCSM condition-1 extensions remain open under #481's survey (growth first — its anchor averages the whole outcome series, the most exposed case); they are not #390 items.

## P3 — reason-partitioned row exclusions

#419 built the mechanism (`PreparedData.dropped_by_reason`, loader drops recorded under `"loader"`, `factories._subset` accumulating labelled drops summing to `dropped_rows`) — but the did factory still subset with the default label, so a reviewed `did-001` config read `{loader: 1, factory_stage: 56}` while its own analysis contract distinguished 53 design-excluded rows from 3 missing outcomes. Both did branches now subset in **two labelled steps** — rows outside the modelled waves/periods leave as `design_excluded` (the levels frame carries t4, which the model does not fit), rows with an unobserved outcome (or, in the dose branch, missing sessions/baseline) leave as `missing_data` — and a factory test asserts the components are disjoint, reconcile to both `dropped_rows` and the source row count, and match independently computed expectations. `did-101`, fitted after the change, records `{loader: 1, design_excluded: 53, missing_data: 3}` in agreement with the contract's independent `loader_dropped_rows`/`design_excluded_rows`/`factory_missing_excluded_rows` fields. Existing artefacts keep the old single label until their next refit — the contract fields, which were already partitioned, remain the audit source for those.
