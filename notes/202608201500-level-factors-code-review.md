## Finding 1 (statistical, moderate): the balance term `arm_gap_t1` is prior-dominated, so the #552 "imbalance is subtracted out" claim is only partially delivered — and nothing in the robustness machinery measures how partially

Under the t1-referenced parameterisation the randomised headline is `d_grp_time[t2] = b_grp[t2] − arm_gap_t1`, and the report says the chance t1 imbalance "is removed rather than carried into the estimate" (`_results_factors.qmd` lines 115–119 and 174–176; the key-findings causal sentence says it is "taken out", `reporting.py::_kf_build_level_factors` ~line 5419). That subtraction is only as real as the posterior of `arm_gap_t1` — and the fitted evidence says that posterior is substantially the prior's doing, not the data's:

- `arm_gap_t1` carries the cross-coupling prior `Normal(0, 0.3)` (`factories.py` line 4908; `priors.py::gamma_cross_prior`) — tighter than the focal `d_grp_time` prior (0.5 proximal / 0.3 distal) — while the per-wave `d_grp_time[t]` priors additionally pool it toward the cross-wave average gap (acknowledged in `notes/202608191900-level-factors-t1-gap-reference.md`, "two things to know", item 1).
- In **8 of the 11** reporting fits (`lf-001/004/005/006/007/009/010/011`), `psense_summary.csv` flags `arm_gap_t1` as "potential strong prior / weak likelihood" (likelihood power-scaling sensitivity 0.014–0.046 — the data barely move it); the same 8 flag "potential prior-data conflict" on `d_grp_time[t2]`. Only `lf-002/003/008` are clear on both.
- The #552 note's own `test`-preset check showed the mechanism concretely: `lf-001`'s stored free-fit adjusted t1 gap of −0.17 became `arm_gap_t1` = +0.03 under the new parameterisation, i.e. the balance estimate largely vanished into the prior, and `d_grp_time[t2]` (+0.34) landed between the raw t2 gap and the trace-derived difference (+0.42).

Mechanically: with the likelihood this weak on the gaps, shrinking `arm_gap_t1` toward zero pushes `d_grp_time[t2]` toward the **raw** adjusted t2 gap — the quantity #552 was adopted to stop reporting. Because the adjusted t1 gaps are negative on every outcome in these data, the leak is currently _conservative_ (it attenuates the benefit estimate), so no released headline is overstated; but that is a fact about this sample's imbalance direction, not a property of the design, and it would flip anti-conservative if a t1 gap were positive.

The gap in the machinery: the release gate watches only the focal term's psense row, and the registered sweep (`scripts/level_factors_prior_sensitivity.py`) moves only `tau_prior_sigma` — the `d_grp_time` prior. No registered artefact varies the **balance prior**, which is the lever that directly trades off against the causal contrast. Recommendations, in order of value:

1. Add a balance-prior axis to the level sweep (or a one-off companion per outcome): refit with `arm_gap_t1` on, say, `Normal(0, 0.3)` vs `Normal(0, 1.0)` and report how `d_grp_time[t2]` moves. The factory already takes `tau_prior_sigma`; an analogous `arm_gap_prior_sigma` passthrough is a small change.
2. Soften the two reader-facing "subtracted out / taken out" sentences (partial + key-findings builder) to say the model _estimates and subtracts_ the t1 gap, and that this estimate is itself regularised — in most fits strongly — toward zero by its prior, so the correction is partial at this sample size. The balance block already half-says this ("reported with its own prior"); the causal sentences should not say more than the balance block does.
3. Consider whether the balance term should carry a _wider_ prior than the focal term rather than a tighter one: a nuisance whose mis-shrinkage leaks into the causal contrast is the one place under-regularisation is cheaper than over-regularisation. (The DiD family shares the idiom, so any change should be made or declined for both, with the reasoning recorded.)

## Finding 2 (statistical, minor): the time-invariant `gamma_grp_ability` is baseline composition under the t1 reference, but the AME nets it out while keeping `arm_gap_t1` in

`gamma_grp_ability` multiplies `G × ability` at **all four waves**, including pre-randomisation t1 — under the same logic that motivated #552 for the arm main effect, its t1 component is chance imbalance in the arm–ability slope, pooled with any post-randomisation moderation. Two consequences:

- In `level_t2_marginal_effect` (reporting.py lines 4139–4160), the untreated baseline `eta0` for treated rows _keeps_ the time-invariant `arm_gap_t1` (correct: pre-existing composition) but _removes_ the equally time-invariant interaction term. That is internally inconsistent as a counterfactual — though the practical impact is second-order: the logit-scale contrast added back is unchanged, and only the expit operating points of treated rows shift slightly.
- The reported `gamma_grp_ability` "association" row conflates baseline slope imbalance with moderation. The report already fences it ("identified from all four timepoints, three of them not randomised") and the causal card excludes it (#271 item 5), so nothing released is wrong — but the open #389 finding 1 estimand review should consider a wave-varying or t1-centred interaction alongside the population-vs-profile question it already carries.

## Finding 3 (functional, minor): LF `rope_summary.csv` still carries the retired 90 % band, and the report displays it

`pipelines/level_factors.py` (~line 269) calls the external `rope_card` directly, whereas the gain/ITT path goes through `reporting.py::rope_summary`, which strips the `*_lo90`/`*_hi90` columns per the 2026-07-17 credible-interval standard ("so the raw rope table matches the median + 50 % + 89 % convention everywhere it surfaces"). All eleven fitted LF `rope_summary.csv` files therefore contain `tau_logit_lo90/hi90` and `items_lo90/hi90`, and the partial renders the full transposed row (`_rope.T`, `_results_factors.qmd` line 309), so the retired band is visible in every level report. Fix: apply the same `_is90` strip in the LF pipeline (or lift the strip into a shared helper both families call). Downstream readers (`_kf_headline_from_rope`, `rope_markdown`) use named 50/89 columns, so nothing mis-reads — this is a convention breach, not a numerical error.

## Finding 4 (functional, latent): a pooled (`group_by_time=False`) level fit would be spuriously withheld by the release gate

`release.gate_applies` covers every `level_factors` fit, and `causal_term_for` falls back to `"b_grp_time[1]"` when the plan's `focal_term` is falsy (release.py lines 208–211). That fallback is right for stored pre-#552 fits (key absent) but wrong for a post-#552 **pooled** fit (key present, value `None`, posterior contains only `beta_grp`): the gate would look for a psense row that structurally cannot exist and withhold fail-closed — exactly the "structurally not present ≠ not measured" failure the gain-family treated-only exemption fixes (release.py lines 155–164). No registered LF model is pooled, so this is latent; but `gate_applies` should exempt an LF plan whose `focal_term` is explicitly `None`, mirroring the gain family, before anyone registers a pooled comparator.

## Finding 5 (documentation, trivial): stale pipeline comment says F/T are skipped from the ROPE card

The comment in `pipelines/level_factors.py` (~lines 197–201) says the card covers "ROPE_DELTA → W/R/E/L/B" and "F/T (no agreed delta) are still skipped". Since the ½-natural-maturation δ ratifications (F/T adopted 2026-07-20, ratified 2026-08-19; TR/TE already present), `ROPE_DELTA` carries all graded LF outcomes and the code emits cards for every one — confirmed in the fitted dirs (`lf-007`/`lf-008` have `rope_summary.csv` and key findings). The behaviour is correct; the comment should be updated so no one "fixes" the code to match it.

## Finding 6 (report prose, minor): "`arm_gap_t1` … is in both arms' prediction" is literally false

`arm_gap_t1` multiplies `G`, so it appears only in the **immediate arm's** linear predictor. What the code actually does (correctly) is keep it in both the with- and without-treatment predictions of each treated row — it is never added or removed, so it cancels from the logit contrast. The wrong phrasing appears in the reader-facing partial (`_results_factors.qmd` line 213, "which is in both arms' prediction"), the `level_t2_marginal_effect` docstring and inline comment (reporting.py ~4080/4155), the test docstring (`test_reporting.py` ~1426), and the #552 note. The partial is the one that matters — an adversarial reader will check the algebra and find the sentence wrong even though the computation is right. Suggested wording: "which stays in the prediction both with and without the t2 change, so it is not part of the effect".

## Finding 7 (limitation, minor): six of the eleven LF fits have no sweep path if they ever go prior-dominant

`LEVEL_SENSITIVITY_MODEL_IDS` (sensitivity.py lines 592–599) supports only W/L/P/B/N, and the runner errors on other outcomes. A prior-dominant classification on R/E/F/T/TR/TE would withhold with a named remedy ("a tau_prior_sensitivity.csv treatment-prior sweep") that the tooling cannot produce. Currently moot — all eleven released under the conflict/clear classes, which need no sweep — but extending the map (with a distal-tier grid for R/E/F/T, whose registered τ scale is 0.3 and for whom the proximal 0.25/0.5/0.75 grid is mis-centred) would close it.

## Finding 8 (report rendering, trivial): empty "What the model predicts on the test scale" section in every LF report

The shared partial prints that heading unconditionally (`_results_factors.qmd` line 322), but only the gain family writes `predicted_scores.csv` / `treatment_marginal.csv`; LF dirs have neither, so every level report renders the heading with no content. Gate the heading on artefact presence like the blocks beneath it.

## Finding 9 (labelling, trivial): under the free comparator, the t1 element would be mislabelled

`factor_summary_roles` adds the `balance` role only under the t1 reference; a `arm_gap_reference="free"` fit would label `b_grp_time[0]` — the pre-randomisation t1 gap — as an ordinary `association`. No free comparator is registered, and the priors table documents the free vector conservatively, so this only matters if the comparator is ever fitted for the side-by-side the docs promise.

## Verified sound (checked, no issue)

- **Plan discipline:** settings validated before `make_context` resets the output dir or data load (unknown keys, mixed typed/extra, `group_ability` without ability, `t1` reference on a pooled term all fail early); the resolved plan is the single source for coefficient names, diag vars, focal term, psense labels, gate term and recipe — confirmed consistent end-to-end in the fitted `config.json` / `psense_summary.csv` / `release_decision.json` (focal rows present and read; e.g. `lf-001` released as `prior_data_conflict` with the attenuation note).
- **Identification guards:** `validate_prepared` requires both arms among t2 rows with an observed outcome and finite ability on fitted rows, mirroring the factory keep-mask; the factory rejects a non-four-wave panel under the t1 reference and anchors from t1 rows only (Haldane-smoothed, arm-blind, mover-rate for the off-floor likelihood).
- **Covariate timing (#247 / review finding A1):** `split_confounders_by_timing` routes `deapp_c`/`erbto` (+ indicators) to the t1 baseline broadcast and keeps `hs` contemporaneous per wave; `prepare_kwargs` wires this correctly, and the eleven modules' `adjust_for` sets match the gain family outcome-for-outcome against the revised DAG. No measure-skill adjusters (post-treatment mediators) — correct for a levels model.
- **AME algebra:** under the t1 reference only `d_grp_time[t2]` is netted out and added back; the balance term is untouched, the interaction is excluded (effect at mean ability, declared), and the free comparator reproduces the raw t2 gap — pinned by `test_level_t2_marginal_effect_t1_referenced_adds_back_only_the_t2_change` and reproduced by hand.
- **Intercepts (#389 finding 2):** the t1-anchored `alpha` + exact zero-sum `alpha_time` removes the translation ridge; the `alpha_time_prior_sigma=0.75` calibration arithmetic (ZeroSumNormal marginal SD `σ·√3/2` for four waves; ±0.85 logits ≈ 1.3 SD) checks out.
- **Loader fail-loud checks:** duplicate `(subject, time)` keys, within-subject group changes, non-integer/negative/above-ceiling counts, constant-covariate dropping with `effective_adjustment` reconciliation — all present on the levels path.
- **Prior pushforward:** the level family's estimand-scale prior check goes through the same t2 net-out transform as the posterior card, guarded so a missing `prior` group degrades to a warning after sampling rather than losing the fit.
- **Sweep provenance:** the runner binds each cell to the primary's config/trace/data hashes and arm counts, gates on all-free-RV convergence, and stamps level-family provenance on each cell trace.
