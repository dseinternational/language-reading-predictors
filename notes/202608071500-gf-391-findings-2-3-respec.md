# #391 findings 2+3: the gain-factor respecification (interaction-free headlines, off-floor pre indicator, moderation variants)

**Date:** 2026-08-07. **Issue:** #391. **Decision provenance:** the 2026-07-22 decision comment on #391 (findings 2–6), which this change executes for findings 2 and 3 — the two that imply refits — plus the single reporting sweep those findings share. Finding 1 was fixed in #395, finding 4 in #417, finding 6's typed settings in the Phase-3 migration; finding 5 (cross-family comparison on the canonical estimand) is deliberately sequenced after this change settles what the canonical gain-factor estimand is.

## What changed

**Finding 3 — the causal headline is interaction-free.** All 21 pre-existing gain-factor specs (13 primaries, 8 treated-only companions) declared `(("trt", "ability"), ("trt", "own"), ("age", "ability"))`. The treatment interactions were estimated on **all** stacked periods — the post-crossover transitions carry no untreated comparison and treatment-affected baselines — so the interaction-aware period-1 marginal was partly model-dependent extrapolation (the #391 finding 3 bind: netting the interactions out imports post-crossover information; not netting them out is the pre-#395 bug). Every headline spec now declares `(("age", "ability"),)` only. The typed settings layer enforces the invariant both ways: a spec that is not a moderation variant may not declare a `trt` pair (this covers treated-only companions too, keeping each a one-line diff from its parent), and a moderation variant must declare at least one and must not be treated-only. With no `trt` interactions, the period-1 average marginal effect's direction coincides with `beta_trt` draw-by-draw, so the #395 direction fix is a structural no-op for headlines — exactly as the decision comment predicted — while remaining live for the variants.

**The moderation variants (`lrp-rli-gf-201`–`211`, legacy suffix `m`).** The pre-specified moderation questions were part of the original analysis plan, so they are not deleted: each per-outcome primary (gf-001–011) gains an explicitly associational variant that restores `trt × ability` and `trt × own` unchanged. The variants keep the #395 interaction-aware netting (that machinery is what they exist to exercise), label the netted marginal "model-dependent … partly informed by post-crossover data" in the plan, the predicted-scores contrast status and the key-findings box, flag `beta_trt` as an _association_ in `factor_summary.csv`, and are **skipped by the robustness release gate** (`release.gate_applies`) for the same structural reason as the treated-only companions: there is no causal headline here to gate — it lives in the interaction-free primary, which _is_ gated. Two scope decisions worth recording:

- **gf-012/013 take no variants.** They are alternate adjustment sets for TR/TE (#421 Tier 1); the moderation question is per-outcome, and the gf-209/210 variants (anchored to the per-outcome primaries gf-009/010) carry it. Two variants per outcome would duplicate an already-underpowered analysis.
- **The `m` suffix takes the uniform 2xx block** (`lrpgf01m → lrp-rli-gf-201`, …). The lrp77 precedent assigns per-parent alphabetical +100/+200, but gf-009–011 have no `b` companion, so per-parent numbering would scatter one family role across the 1xx and 2xx blocks. One suffix, one block; the explicit `_VARIANT_RENUMBER` table records it.

The variants' own posteriors should be read with the decision comment's caveat attached: moderation on ~25 period-1 control children is not seriously estimable — these fits _bound_ the moderation questions, they do not settle them.

**Finding 2 — the off-floor baseline is the binary off-floor-at-pre indicator.** The decision rejected both prior states: the original spec (no own main effect, `trt × own` fitted) forced the control arm flat in the baseline and let the interaction absorb the real dependence — the period-1 control table (2/17 at-floor vs 7/8 off-floor moved off the floor at post) flatly contradicts the restriction, and the fitted `gamma_int_trt_own` (median 0.804) was main-effect leakage, not effect modification; the interim #413 repair restored a graded standardised-pre-logit main effect, but the pre logit of a heavily-floored measure is a near-degenerate spike. The factory now always carries `gamma_own_offfloor × 1[pre > 0]` on the off-floor path — with or without interactions — and `term_vecs["own"]` _is_ the indicator there, so any variant interaction on `own` shares its functional form and hierarchy holds by construction. The association-marginals row for it is labelled `off-floor at pre (0 to 1)` (a new `AssociationTerm.perturbation_label`), not `+1 SD`.

**The indicator prior: `gamma_own_offfloor ~ Normal(0, 1)`** (new shared constructor in `priors.py`). Rationale, since a future reader might question the scale: the control-table MLE gap is ~4 logits, but it is marginal — in the model, the skills (L, B), ability, age, adjusters and the child intercept share the load. `Normal(0, 0.3)` (the cross-coupling scale the #413 repair used) would crush a known-strong prognostic contrast; `Normal(1, 0.25)` bakes in a graded test–retest calibration that has no published analogue for a binary indicator. `Normal(0, 1)` spans odds ratios to ~7 at 2 SD, regularises against quasi-separation (the off-floor→off-floor cell is 7/8), and does not bake in a direction. The fitted reporting posteriors vindicate the zero-centred choice in both directions: gf-005 (P) recovers a strong positive contrast (median +2.23 logits, 89% CrI +1.41 to +3.06), while gf-011 (N) finds essentially none once the code skills L/B, ability and the adjusters are conditioned on (−0.17, 89% CrI −0.85 to +0.52) — a positive-centred prior would have manufactured an association the N data do not carry.

## Recovery test (acceptance criterion)

`tests/statistical_models/test_gf_statistical_validation.py` fits the production off-floor model (Bernoulli likelihood, always-on indicator main effect, on-intervention term, non-centred child intercept) to a simulated 80-child, 3-period panel with the real sampler (nutpie, 2 × 500/500). The generating `gamma_own_offfloor = 2.0` is recovered (mean within 0.5, 94% interval covers, P(>0) > 0.99), `beta_trt = 0.6` is covered (the N(0, 0.5) tier prior shrinks the point estimate at this sample size, so coverage is the honest assertion), the combined period-1 intercept and the realised child-intercept spread are covered, and the outcome-independent age term is null. Under the pre-decision specification this signal had nowhere to go but the interaction — the test pins the repair.
