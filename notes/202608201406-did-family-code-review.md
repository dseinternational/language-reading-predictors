# DiD family code review: findings and fixes

> [!NOTE]
> Drafted by a LLM-based AI tool (Claude Code/Fable 5).

**Date:** 2026-08-20. **Scope:** a full functional and statistical review of the difference-in-differences family — the typed settings and run plan (`statistical_models/did.py`), the factory (`build_did_model`, both the arm-by-wave and dose branches), the loader paths it consumes (`load_and_prepare` in `levels` and `all` modes, including the `attend` interval-covariate semantics), the family pipeline (`pipelines/did.py`), the reporting layer (`did_summary`, `did_cell_ppc`, the prior pushforwards), the shared dose-slope writer, the figure artefacts, the priors-table roles, the release-gate focal-term logic, the key-findings builder, the cross-model comparison script, and all eighteen registered model modules. The review cross-checked the design coding against `data/rli_data_long.csv` directly, and the family's three test files all pass, including the NUTS parameter-recovery test that samples the real factory.

**Overall verdict:** the family is sound. The model mathematics, likelihoods, data handling and causal labelling discipline are correct. The review found one genuine artefact-correctness bug (finding 1), two estimand-coherence issues (findings 2 and 3), one internal-consistency gap in a summary (finding 4), and a handful of minor observations. Findings 1, 3 and 4 are fixed in the change accompanying this note; finding 2 is a wording correction plus a recorded open question; the rest are recorded with no code change.

## Finding 1 — wrong dose scaler persisted by the DiD dose companions (fixed)

`write_dose_slope_summary` (`pipelines/dose_response.py`) persisted `dose_mean_sessions` / `dose_sd_sessions` from `ctx.prepared.covariate_scalers["attend"]` — the loader's standardiser, computed over **all** loaded transition rows including the waitlist arm's zero-session P1 rows and the P3 rows. That is correct for the dose_response family, whose factory fits the loader-standardised dose directly. It is wrong for the DiD dose companions: `build_did_model`'s dose branch deliberately re-standardises sessions **among treated P1/P2 rows only** and fits its slopes per 1 SD of that scale, recording the treated-rows scaler in `DidDosePayload.dose_scaler` and in `config.json`'s `dose_standardization` block. On the real data the two scales differ materially (loader ≈ mean 53.5 / SD 31.0; treated-rows ≈ mean 70.1 / SD 17.1), so `lrp-rli-did-007`'s `dose_slope_summary.csv` misstated its per-session calibration by roughly 1.8× and contradicted the same fit's own `config.json`. Blast radius was contained: only did-007 reaches this writer from `fit_did`, and `mediation_calibration.py` consumes only `lrp-rli-dose-*` fits, where the loader scaler is the right one. **Fix:** the writer takes an optional `dose_scaler`; `fit_did` passes the fitted payload's treated-rows scaler; a unit test pins the behaviour for both paths.

## Finding 2 — `tau_t2` is the adjusted t2 arm gap, not literally a difference-in-differences (wording fixed; estimand question recorded)

The arm-by-wave model estimates free per-wave gaps, so the parameter named `tau_t2` is the covariate-adjusted t2 arm-gap **level**, not the differenced quantity `tau_t2 - arm_gap_t1`. The family's own validation tests treat it exactly that way (the saturated-MLE checks compute it as the plain t2 cell-logit difference), yet the module docstring and the resolved plan's estimand text called it "the clean randomised t2 difference-in-differences contrast". Under randomisation the t2 gap level is a perfectly valid causal contrast, so nothing was wrong with the estimate — but the name promised a differencing that does not happen. Two things are worth having on the record. First, the baseline adjustment that does occur is **soft and prior-weighted**: a realised t1 imbalance is allocated between `arm_gap_t1` (tight Normal(0, 0.3) prior) and the arm-mean of the shared child random intercepts, and whatever the intercepts absorb is netted out of all three wave gaps, ANCOVA-style — so `tau_t2` sits between the unadjusted t2 gap and the fully baseline-corrected one, with the mix set by `sigma_child` and the `arm_gap_t1` prior scale (the tight `arm_gap_t1` prior works in our favour here, pushing realised imbalance into the intercepts). Second, there is now a cross-family asymmetry: after #552 the level-factor family's causal headline is `d_grp_time[t2]` — an actual gap-change (difference-in-differences) estimand — while the family carrying "DiD" in its name reports the gap level. Both are defensible randomised estimands and they differ only when the realised t1 gap is non-zero. **Fix applied:** the docstrings and the resolved-plan estimand/causal-status text now say "randomised t2 arm contrast (the covariate-adjusted t2 arm-gap level)" and state the soft-adjustment mechanism. **Open question for sign-off:** whether the DiD family should also be re-parameterised on the t1-referenced gap-change, mirroring #552, or whether keeping the two families on deliberately different randomised estimands is the more informative triangulation. No re-parameterisation was done in this change.

## Finding 3 — the DiD dose marginal averaged over untreated rows (fixed)

The natural-scale `dose_marginal_summary` ("+1 SD of session dose on every fitted row") averaged over **all** fitted rows, including the waitlist-P1 rows where the dose is structurally zero and treatment presence is off — an operating point at which a dose increment is not a supported counterfactual in the DiD design (the `theta_treated` presence term is not toggled). The coherent averaging population for an intensive-margin-among-treated estimand is the treated rows. For the dose_response family the all-rows average is the intended definition (its zero-dose rows anchor the slope at dose = 0 on a common standardised scale), so the writer keeps that default. **Fix:** `write_dose_slope_summary` takes an optional `marginal_row_mask`; `fit_did` passes the payload's treated-row mask for both the period-varying marginal and the matching prior pushforward, and the pooled DiD dose companions' inline pushforward takes the same mask, so the prior check and the posterior marginal stay on one transform and one population. The `dose_marginal_summary` table now also records `n_rows` and `row_population`, so the averaging population is self-describing. The prior-sensitivity sweep runner (`scripts/did_prior_sensitivity.py`), which mirrors the marginal's definition when translating sweep cells to the items scale, was updated in lockstep.

## Finding 4 — the varying-delta model published a partially-integrated t3 items summary (fixed)

For `lrp-rli-did-013` (waitlist catch-up heterogeneity), `did_summary` already withholds `delta_crossover_items` because a scalar arm-gap toggle cannot integrate the fitted child-specific catch-up deviations — but it still published `arm_gap_t3_items_*` and the t3 arm-mean columns computed from `eta_base`, which excludes those same fitted `v_delta` deviations, so the "waitlist t3 level" misstated what the model actually fitted. The same disqualifying logic applies to every t3 outcome-scale quantity in that variant. **Fix:** when `delta_crossover_i` is in the posterior, `did_summary` now omits the t3 standardised block entirely and records `arm_gap_t3_items_available = False` with an omission reason (the t1/t2 quantities are unaffected — `v_delta` enters only waitlist t3 rows); the report partial prints the omission explicitly rather than leaving a silent absence (#381's meta-finding that a missing artefact reads as a clean one). The logit-scale `arm_gap_t3` posterior is unaffected and still reported.

## Minor observations (no code change, or docstring only)

**Cell-PPC flag thresholds.** `did_cell_ppc`'s tail flags use fixed 2.5% / 97.5% thresholds (a 95% two-sided convention) while the interval columns render at the house `ci_prob = 0.89`. Deliberate, but previously unstated; the docstring now says so. The flags themselves are unchanged.

**Fail-loud phase-zero broadcast.** `_broadcast_phase_zero` raises (killing the fit) if any subject in the prepared frame lacks a phase-zero row, rather than dropping the child. Verified that it cannot fire on the current data: all 54 children have complete t1 rows (group, age, attend), every child has all four wave rows, and the only missing age in the file is one t4 cell outside the DiD design. Acceptable fail-loud posture; recorded so a future data delivery that masks a t1 row is understood as a crash-not-drop.

**Legacy `extra` declarations.** All eighteen DiD model modules still declare settings through `extra` rather than typed `DiDModelSettings`; the strict `from_legacy_extra` translator rejects unknown keys before any directory reset, so this is safe, but every fit records `settings_source="legacy_extra"`. Migration to the typed declarations is a mechanical follow-up, deferred.

**`beta_period` prior scale.** The binary model's wave offsets take `tau_prior()` (Normal(0, 0.5)). Maturation over 20–40 weeks on proximal outcomes could plausibly exceed that scale; because the arm gaps are within-wave contrasts, `tau_t2` is largely insensitive, but shrunk wave offsets shift the operating points behind the standardised items numbers. Recorded as a question — a prior change needs its own sign-off and refit, and is not made here.

**Catalogue staleness.** The CLAUDE.md family bullet's "LRP-RLI-DID-001–013" range predates did-014/015 (the APT expressive information/grammar fits); the file itself already directs readers to `docs/models/README.md` and the registry as authoritative, so no edit was made.

## Verified sound (for the record)

The `treated = (G == 1) | (phase == 1)` coding matches the observed session data cell-for-cell (immediate arm dosed in both periods, waitlist only in P2, waitlist-P1 recorded as zero sessions, so the untreated cell survives the complete-case mask). The loader's fail-loud checks (duplicate keys, within-subject group changes, count-ceiling and integer guards, the G = 2 − group sign convention) are correct. The treated-centred dose standardisation is internally consistency-checked in the factory. The AME machinery nets `tau_t2·G` out correctly under the t2 row mask, and the predicted-scores path applies the row mask to `child_idx` before indexing the child effects. The convergence gate widens beyond the curated diagnostic variables to all free RVs; psense covers the variant-defining terms (did-007's `sigma_dose` / `beta_dose_phase`, did-013's `sigma_delta`); the release gate reads the focal term from the persisted plan. The empirical-Bayes intercept anchor has its registered independent-prior companion (did-101), the wide-prior companion (did-102) widens only `tau_t2` and correctly disqualifies `delta_crossover` as a comparison target, and the key-findings, priors-table and report-partial prose keep the causal/associational split honest throughout.
