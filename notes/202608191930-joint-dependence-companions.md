# Dependence-aware companions of the joint taught-versus-not-taught contrasts (#551)

**Date:** 2026-08-19. **Decision (issue #551, confirmed by the author on 2026-08-19):** the three two-outcome `joint` contrasts — `lrp-rli-itt-015` (`TE − UE`), `lrp-rli-itt-115` (`TR − UR`) and `lrp-rli-itt-016` (`TE − TR`) — get registered sensitivity companions with the per-child LKJ residual-correlation block **on**, so that each contrast interval is dependence-aware. This note records the implementation choices; the scientific rationale (the parents factorise, so each contrast's interval is the independent sum of two AME variances and omits the within-child covariance the same 54 children induce — too wide under a positive residual correlation, too narrow under a negative one, point estimate unaffected) is in the issue and in `notes/202608181002-findings-07-joint.md`. The three `reporting` fits are part of the post-review batch (#554) and had not been run when this note was written; nothing here is a result.

## What was registered

- **Ids.** `lrp-rli-itt-215` (of 015), `lrp-rli-itt-315` (of 115) and `lrp-rli-itt-216` (of 016): a uniform **+200** block for the dependence-sensitivity role, because the +100 slot of 015 is already its receptive `b` companion (115) and one role should live in one block (the `gf` `m` variants took the 2xx block for the same reason). Registry entries carry `base=` pointing at the parent and name the available-case modified ITT estimand.
- **Specs.** Each companion module derives its settings from the parent's `SPEC` with `replace` from the standard-library `dataclasses` module (`use_residual_correlation=True`, `joint_structure="residual_correlated"`, a companion `dependence_note`), so outcomes, precision terms, `loo_unit` and contrast metadata cannot drift apart; a test checks field-by-field equality with the parent apart from the block and the note. The parents' `dependence_note` now names their companion and says the contrast is dependence-checked only once the companion has passed the house gate.
- **What the block reports.** `JointRunPlan.diagnostic_vars()` adds `sigma_outcome` and the new Deterministic `u_corr_pair` (the strictly-lower-triangle correlations as scalars, coordinate `outcome_pair`, e.g. `UE|TE`) when the block is on, and `JointRunPlan.psense_vars` covers them beside `tau`. The scalar pairs exist because the full `u_corr` matrix carries a constant unit diagonal that breaks the density-based posterior, prior-vs-posterior and psense figures (seen on the first dev fit). `priors_table.csv` documents `u_chol` and `u_z` with rationales. `_results_joint.qmd` branches on the persisted plan's `use_residual_correlation`: a companion describes itself as a dependence-aware posterior difference and prints a "Within-child residual dependence" table (`sigma_outcome`, `u_corr_pair`) with the weak-identification reading; a parent keeps the factorised caveat and points at its companion. The same edit fixes a latent defect: the partial read the joint outcome list from the legacy `spec_extra`, which is empty for typed settings, so the parents' "Additional outcome-specific predictive plots" callout rendered empty although the second outcome's plots were on disk.
- **Compute check.** A `dev`-preset fit of `lrp-rli-itt-215` runs end to end: `compute_log_prior` and power scaling work with `pm.LKJCholeskyCov` (the #453 concern was bare `pm.LKJCorr`), the gate scans `u_chol` / `u_z`, and the contrast median (+0.036) matched the stored parent (+0.034) to Monte Carlo error. A dev fit proves nothing about convergence at the reporting preset: the earlier two-outcome attempt mixed poorly because a per-outcome residual SD sat at its zero boundary, and under the unrounded house gate a repeat withholds the result — the follow-up is then a paired bootstrap / randomisation sensitivity outside the pipeline, to be opened as its own issue.
