# Pooled-levels between/within split extended to the other predictors of word reading (#553)

**Date:** 2026-08-19. **Decision (issue #553, confirmed by the author on 2026-08-19):** extend the `pooled_levels` family's Mundlak between/within split from letter sounds (`lrp-rli-pl-001`) to expressive vocabulary, receptive vocabulary, word/nonword repetition and speech production as four registered fits, `lrp-rli-pl-003` to `-006`, all with word reading as the outcome. This note records the implementation choices; the scientific rationale — the split is the single most informative distinction for the "does it contribute" questions (question-organised report, questions 6 and 7), and speech production is where it changes what the notes can say — is in the issue. The four `reporting` fits are part of the post-review batch (#554) and had not been run when this note was written; nothing here is a result.

## Two family extensions, both copying existing idioms

- **Raw-score covariate exposures** (`mechanism_is_covariate`, the `mechanism` family's route, `lrp_rli_mech_090.py`). `erbto` and `deapp_c` are integer scores whose documented maxima are recorded nowhere, so registering them as bounded-count measures would fabricate a denominator. They enter as the loader's standardised raw score, re-standardised on the fitted rows (+1 SD is one SD of the exposure actually fitted; the raw-units SD and mean are recorded beside the fit as `mechanism_exposure_sd_raw` / `_mean_raw`, the mechanism family's convention, and quoted in the key findings and the report). The exposure must be named in `require_observed`, so the mean-imputed child-wave rows are dropped before fitting — imputation plus an indicator is an adjuster policy, never acceptable for the exposure itself — and the number of rows that filter removes is recovered by loading the same frame without the filter and differencing (the loader does not report it) and recorded as `n_dropped_require_observed_rows`. The Mundlak split is unchanged; both scores are measured at every wave, so the within-child term is meaningful. Resolution rejects, before any output directory is reset or data are loaded: a bounded measure declared raw, a raw symbol the loader cannot enforce `require_observed` for (only `hs` / `erbto` / `deapp_c` carry a missingness flag), a raw exposure not in `require_observed`, and a raw exposure also listed in `adjust_for` or as the ability covariate; the mechanism family's missing-covariate policy check is reused rather than copied.
- **Same-wave skill adjusters** (`skill_symbols`, the `concurrent` family's idiom). Other bounded-count measures at the row's wave enter as standardised logits (the same clipped-proportion transform the family's own bounded exposure uses), each a `gamma_<symbol>` on the cross-coupling prior; they load as further outcomes of the levels frame, and a row is kept only when the outcome, the exposure and every skill are observed at that wave (the dropped count is reported and stated in the report). Unlike `concurrent`, missing skills are not mean-imputed. Resolution rejects the outcome or the exposure as a skill, an unknown symbol, a repeat, and a bounded measure placed in `adjust_for` instead of `skill_symbols`. The effective-adjustment record lists them as `measure` terms at `same_wave`, and the priors table documents them as contemporaneous, possibly post-treatment levels (Table-2 fallacy) rather than under the hearing / speech adjuster text.

## The four fits

Adjustment sets mirror each exposure's `mechanism` model minus the own baseline, with `blocks` as the t1 ability proxy broadcast across waves and linear age; `attend` is omitted throughout, as in `pl-001` (an interval dose is a transition covariate, not a level). `pl-003` (E → W): hearing, phonological memory and speech with their indicators, skills `TR`, `TE`, `R`. `pl-004` (R → W): hearing and phonological memory, skill `TR`. `pl-005` (`erbto` → W, raw covariate, `require_observed=("erbto",)`): hearing. `pl-006` (`deapp_c` → W, raw covariate, `require_observed=("deapp_c",)`): hearing and phonological memory. All use typed `PooledLevelsModelSettings`; the plan records `exposure_kind` (`bounded_count` / `raw_covariate`), `require_observed` and `skill_symbols`, and the recipe describes the exposure correctly for either kind.

Dev-preset pipeline checks (not results): `pl-003` fitted 210 child-wave rows from 53 children with 5 rows dropped for a missing skill or exposure; `pl-005` dropped 8 imputed-`erbto` rows and fitted 202 rows, with +1 SD of the fitted ERB total = 9.47 raw points. The optional `exposure_lag` setting the issue's comment mentions (prior-wave exposure with the outcome's prior-wave level as an adjuster) was **not** implemented: it is explicitly optional and low priority, the expected answer is a fourth inconclusive within-child term, and it changes the row construction of the levels frame; it can be added later if wanted.
